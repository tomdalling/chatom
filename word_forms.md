# Astu word forms — final

Final forms for every closed-class word in `astu.md`, plus the non-normative
sample vocabulary used in its examples. Settled 2026-11-03 (Q106, Q123–Q126).

Every form obeys the shape rules: consonant + vowel/glide syllables inside a
word, at most one final consonant, never a final `w` or `r`. Open-class words
never begin with a reserved prefix syllable (`pu`, `vu`, `ru`). A word's class
comes from the dictionary, not from its form. The style is pseudo-Jamaican;
the **Source** column gives the Patois (or Creole) origin where there is one.

## Pronouns and grammatical words

`mi` I · `iu` you · `dei` he/she/it · `midem` we · `iudem` you (pl) ·
`deidem` they · `dem` plural · `ia` this · `dea` that · `bin` past ·
`fina` future · `am` progressive · `no` negation / question · `ie` yes ·
`be` copula · `fi` possessive
